# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard: Attrition Insights

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition using Power BI to uncover key patterns and factors influencing employee turnover. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of workforce demographics, attrition trends, and critical drivers such as job satisfaction, overtime, salary, and work-life balance.

The goal of this project is to enable organizations to make data-driven decisions to reduce attrition and improve employee retention.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall employee attrition trends
* Identify key factors contributing to employee turnover
* Understand workforce demographics
* Provide actionable insights to improve retention

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 🔹 Executive Overview

* Total Employees
* Attrition Count & Rate
* Average Monthly Income
* Average Job Satisfaction

### 🔹 Workforce Demographics

* Employee distribution by Age, Gender, Job Role, and Marital Status

### 🔹 Attrition Drivers

* Job Satisfaction
* Work-Life Balance
* Salary Level
* Overtime

### 🔹 Insights & Recommendations

* Key findings derived from analysis
* Actionable strategies to reduce attrition

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Employees with low job satisfaction show higher attrition rates
* Overtime significantly increases employee turnover
* Lower salary bands experience higher attrition
* Work-life balance has a noticeable impact on retention

---

## 💡 Recommendations

* Improve employee engagement and satisfaction programs
* Reduce excessive overtime through better workload planning
* Revise compensation strategies for lower salary groups
* Promote flexible work policies to enhance work-life balance
